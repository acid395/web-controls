About the data

Where the numbers come from

End-of-month storage from the Bureau of Reclamation's 24-Month Study. Capacities are live storage, excluding dead pool. Figures here are illustrative and rounded.

What "percent of capacity" means

Current live storage divided by the reservoir's live capacity. A reservoir at 39% holds a little under two fifths of what it could.
