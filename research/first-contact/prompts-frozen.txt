nass-quickstats  https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/
chain    select the survey program, the crops sector and the field crops group
chain    choose corn as the commodity and state as the geographic level, then select 2022 as the year
act      search for soybeans
chain    pick the census program, the animals & products sector and cattle as the commodity, then click get data
chain    select the environmental sector and then clear the selections
chain    set the geographic level to county and the year to 2017
act      choose national as the geographic level
explain  how many records are currently available
explain  what sectors can I choose from
explain  what does this page let me do

eia-electricity  https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/browser/
act      change the data set to retail sales of electricity
chain    switch the table to annual and show it as a column chart
act      set the chart indexing to index to start as percent
chain    change the data set to average retail price of electricity, then switch to quarterly
act      choose the report 1.14 net generation from wind by state by sector
act      open the map view
act      turn on the relative standard errors checkbox
explain  what was net generation from all fuels in july 2026
explain  which energy sources are shown in the chart

prism-explorer  https://prism.oregonstate.edu/explorer/
chain    set latitude to 40 and longitude to -105, then click update location
chain    turn on maximum temp and minimum temp, and turn off mean temp
chain    choose annual values from 2000 to 2020
chain    switch units to si (metric) and resolution to 800m
chain    select daily values, start on 05 march 2020 and end on 10 march 2020
chain    turn on interpolate grid cell values and retrieve the time series
act      hide the text

explain  what is the elevation of the current location
